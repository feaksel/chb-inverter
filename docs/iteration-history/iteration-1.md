# Iteration 1 — single dual-bridge layout, IRFZ44N, IPD LS-PWM

> **Status:** superseded. Not for fabrication. KiCad files for iteration 1 are not preserved in the repo; the closest survivor is the v3.1 BOM at [`hardware/legacy/iteration-3/CHB_BOM_v3_1.xlsx`](https://github.com/feaksel/chb-inverter/blob/main/hardware/legacy/iteration-3/CHB_BOM_v3_1.xlsx).

## What was attempted

A **single PCB that hosted both H-bridges** of the 5-level cascade, with:

- 8× **IRFZ44N** MOSFETs (4 per bridge) in TO-220 packages, per the [Build Guide v3.1](https://github.com/feaksel/chb-inverter/blob/main/docs/assets/pdfs/CHB_Inverter_Build_Guide_v3_1.docx) BOM.
- **TLP250** optical gate drivers on every gate (this part was right from the start).
- **IPD Level-Shifted Carrier PWM** at 500 Hz, generated by the STM32 Nucleo-F303RE.
- Three independent MCP3201 ADCs (DC1, DC2, current), each with its own MISO line back to the controller (the firmware's original "3-MISO" assumption).

The intent was the simplest-possible 5-level inverter — one board, one bring-up, and the most-textbook modulation strategy.

## What failed

| Failure | What happened | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **MOSFET V<sub>DSS</sub> too tight** | IRFZ44N V<sub>DSS</sub> = 55 V; bench supplies at 50 V plus switching spikes brought peak V<sub>DS</sub> uncomfortably close to breakdown. | At a 50 V bus the headroom is barely 10 % nominal; spikes from gate-loop parasitics ate that margin. |
| **TVS / V<sub>DSS</sub> mismatch** | 1.5KE62A TVS clamps at 84.5 V — **above** IRFZ44N V<sub>DSS</sub>. A TVS firing event would still damage the MOSFETs. | Latent BOM hazard. The TVS was sized for a higher-V<sub>DSS</sub> MOSFET than what was specified. |
| **Bridge-1 thermal asymmetry** | With IPD LS-PWM, Bridge 1 always carried the inner-band switching (±1 step). One of Bridge 1's MOSFETs ran measurably warmer than its counterparts. | The IPD mapping puts the most-switched bridge in a fixed role for the whole fundamental period. See [PSC vs. LSPWM](../design-notes/psc-vs-lspwm.md). |
| **3-MISO firmware assumption** | The firmware read three MCP3201 MISO lines in parallel during the SPI scan, assuming each ADC had its own dedicated return. | This worked on the iteration-1 layout — the issue surfaced later when the modular per-bridge isolation made the MISO scheme harder to maintain. |

## What was learned

- **Voltage headroom is not optional.** A 10 % margin above nominal bus isn't enough when gate-loop parasitics add tens of volts of ringing. Iteration 4 raised V<sub>DSS</sub> headroom to ≈ 2×.
- **The protection chain has to actually protect.** If the TVS clamps above the MOSFET V<sub>DSS</sub>, a TVS event is still a MOSFET kill. Check the clamp voltage against the MOSFET rating before ordering.
- **Modulator choice has thermal consequences.** Symmetric (PSC) vs. asymmetric (IPD) modulation directly translates to whether the bridges run at the same temperature. This wasn't obvious at design time; the bench measurements made it concrete.

## What changed for iteration 2

- Schematic split into modular sheets (the start of the `Highside_cell` / `Lowside_cell` / `driver_cell` decomposition that survives in the iteration-4 KiCad).
- Gate-drive routing redrawn around bootstrap timing concerns — leading directly into the [bootstrap fundamentals](../design-notes/bootstrap-fundamentals.md) discussion in iteration 2.

The IRFZ44N and the IPD LS-PWM choice were both kept, on the theory that they were "good enough" and the layout was the bigger risk. Both assumptions were reversed by iteration 4.
